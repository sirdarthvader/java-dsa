# Interview Preparation Operating System

This document describes the habits and evidence used throughout the primary
[24-week roadmap](docs/SENIOR_SWE_24_WEEK_ROADMAP.md). The roadmap sets the
topic sequence; this operating system keeps the workload sustainable and makes
progress visible.

## Weekly cadence

- Start with the coaching profile's sustainable schedule. Treat two hours as a
  ceiling for a study day, not a daily minimum.
- Begin around 8–12 focused hours a week. Increase toward 12–15 only after two
  stable weeks, and reduce the load when work or life needs it.
- Schedule five study blocks and one fallback slot during the weekly review.
- A missed session does not create debt. Resume with the next planned block.
- Use the roadmap's phase goals as direction, not as a reason to rush through
  topics that have not stuck yet.

## Minimum viable session

On a difficult day, spend 10 minutes on a previously solved problem: state the
invariant aloud and write one test case. This keeps the restart small without
pretending that a short session is equivalent to a full practice block.

## Problem-solving protocol

1. Restate the input, output, constraints, and edge cases.
2. Describe a brute-force approach and its complexity.
3. Identify the property that enables the optimized approach.
4. Dry-run a non-trivial example before coding.
5. Write clean code without autocomplete during timed practice.
6. Test normal, boundary, duplicate, empty/null (when valid), and large cases.
7. State time and space complexity and one alternative trade-off.
8. Record the failure mode: recognition, idea, implementation, testing, or
   communication.

## Progress and readiness

Use [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) as the single durable record of the current week,
completed sessions, re-solves, and recurring misses. Update it once per week;
avoid maintaining multiple competing logs.

At each six-week checkpoint, review evidence rather than problem count:

- Can you solve representative unseen problems for the phase and explain the
  invariant clearly?
- Are old problems being re-solved from a blank editor after 2, 7, and 21 days?
- Are implementation, testing, or communication misses recurring?
- Can you explain system-design trade-offs and ground leadership stories in
  your actual work?
- Is the next phase's workload sustainable alongside work and personal
  responsibilities?

If a gate is missed, use the next week to repair the weakest skill and adjust
the sequence. Do not restart the whole roadmap.

## Weekly review (20 minutes)

- What did I complete, and what could I retrieve without help?
- What got in the way: task size, unclear next action, energy, distraction, or
  avoidance after difficulty?
- What one process repair will I try next week?
- Which five study blocks and fallback slot will I calendar?
- Which old problems need a +2, +7, or +21-day re-solve?

## Integrity rule

Use AI for preparation, feedback, and mock practice, not for assistance during
a live interview. Follow the current candidate instructions for the specific
interview process.
